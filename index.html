      Burning questions

      FAQ.

      Is Toaster Roaster free?

      Yes — free forever, no sign-up, no ads, no account. Roast away.

      Will it hurt someone's feelings?

      That's the point of a roast — but keep it playful. Mild is safe for anyone; Nuclear is for people who can take a joke. Don't aim it at strangers.

      Where do my favorites go?

      They're saved in your own browser (local storage). Nobody else can see them, and clearing your browser data clears them too.

      Can I share a roast?

      Yes — copy it with one tap, or use Share to send it to any app. The X/Twitter share opens a ready-made post.

      Is there a new roast every day?

      Yes. The Roast of the Day changes at midnight — come back tomorrow for a fresh burn.
